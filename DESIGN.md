# Design System

## Colors
- Primary Green: #2E7D32 (Standard farm green)
- Accent Yellow: #F9A825 (Subtle highlights)
- Background: #F1F8E9 (Light green for freshness)
- Text: #1B5E20 (Dark green for readability)
- White: #FFFFFF

## Typography
- Headings: 'Montserrat', sans-serif (Modern, Clean)
- Body: 'Open Sans', sans-serif (Readable)

## Components
- **Card**: White background, rounded corners (8px), subtle shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.1)`).
- **Button**: Primary Green background, white text, hover effect (scale up slightly).
- **Navbar**: Sticky at top, semi-transparent.

## Layout
- Responsive Grid for Products (1 col on mobile, 2 col on tablet, 3-4 col on desktop).
- Hero Section: Full viewport height or significant height, background image with overlay.
