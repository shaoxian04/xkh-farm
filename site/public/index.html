About Us

Xin Kiar Huat Enterprise is a dedicated agricultural farm specialising in the cultivation and supply of a wide variety of fresh vegetables. Founded by Mr Tan, the farm was built on years of hands-on experience, perseverance, and a strong commitment to quality farming.

Starting from modest beginnings, Mr Tan focused on producing fresh vegetables for local communities, placing great emphasis on consistency, freshness, and ethical farming practices. Through continuous refinement of farming techniques and an unwavering standard of quality, Xin Kiar Huat Enterprise steadily grew into a trusted name among customers and partners. Today, the farm remains committed to delivering safe, fresh, and high-quality vegetables while upholding the values that shaped its foundation.
